# KieDex User Experience Research & Recommendations

## 1. Project Overview

KieDex is a trading platform that provides users with access to futures trading.

This research documents my personal experience using KieDex, focusing on the trading interface, real-time profit and loss (P&L), position closures, Oil consumption, and trading points.

The goal is to identify potential user experience challenges and propose practical improvements that could help users better understand their trading activity.

**Research perspective:** Independent KieDex user.

**Scope:** User experience observations and community-oriented recommendations.

**Important note:** This document reflects personal observations. It is not an official KieDex report, a technical audit, or a representation of all users' experiences.

## 2. User Experience Findings

### 2.1 Real-Time Profit and Loss

During my use of KieDex, the unrealized profit and loss (P&L) displayed for open positions changed in response to market price movements.

This provided immediate visibility into the changing performance of an open position.

### 2.2 Unexpected Position Closures and Liquidation

I experienced instances where positions appeared to close unexpectedly or were liquidated, particularly when trading BTC and ETH pairs.

The exact causes of these events have not been independently verified. Potential explanations should not be assumed without reviewing the relevant position history and transaction details.

### 2.3 Oil Consumption and Trading Points

In some instances I observed that Oil was consumed or depleted after a position closed unexpectedly, while my displayed trading points did not increase.

I waited and refreshed the page, but the points still did not appear to increase.

I no longer have screenshots or saved transaction records to independently verify these incidents. Therefore, this finding is documented as a personal observation rather than a confirmed platform-wide issue.

### 2.4 Community Relevance

I have also encountered similar complaints from other community members. However, I have not collected specific messages, links, or counts to establish how widespread these concerns are.

These observations suggest that clearer transaction history, position-closure explanations, and point-calculation status could help users better understand their trading activity.

## 3. Recommendations

### 3.1 Improve Trading History Transparency

KieDex could consider enhancing its trading history to help users understand what happened when a position closes.

Potential improvements include:

- **Position closure reason:** Display whether a position was closed due to liquidation, stop-loss, manual action, or another identifiable event, where applicable.
- **Closure details:** Show the closure timestamp and execution price.
- **Oil consumption record:** Display how much Oil was consumed by the relevant trading activity.
- **Trading points status:** Show whether the activity qualified for points and provide a clear explanation when points were not awarded, where possible.

### 3.2 Expected Benefits

Clearer trading records could help users:

- Understand why a position was closed.
- Reconcile Oil consumption with their trading activity.
- Check whether eligible activity was credited with points.
- Submit more informative support requests when discrepancies occur.

### 3.3 Suggested Priority

Prioritize a clear, accessible transaction history that connects position closures, Oil consumption, and trading points in one place.

This recommendation is based on personal user experience and is intended to improve transparency. It does not assume that every proposed detail is currently missing from KieDex.

## 4. Limitations & Conclusion

### 4.1 Research Limitations

This research has several limitations:

- The findings are based primarily on personal experience using KieDex.
- No screenshots or saved transaction records are available to independently verify the reported incidents.
- Similar community complaints were observed, but specific messages and supporting links were not collected.
- The technical causes of unexpected position closures, liquidation events, Oil consumption, and missing point updates remain unverified.
- The findings should not be interpreted as representative of all KieDex users.

### 4.2 Conclusion

KieDex provides real-time visibility into the profit and loss of open positions, based on my personal experience.

However, my experience also highlighted questions about unexpected position closures, Oil consumption, and trading points that did not appear to increase after certain incidents.

Improving the transparency of trading history could help users understand position-closure events, reconcile Oil consumption, and verify point eligibility.

This research is intended to contribute constructive, user-focused feedback and encourage clearer communication between the platform and its community.
