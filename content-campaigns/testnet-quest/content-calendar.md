**Campaign Theme:** Explore Web3, one test at a time.  
**Target Audience:** Web3 beginners

## Day-by-Day Direction

### Day 1 — What Is a Testnet?

Explain that a testnet is an environment for experimenting with blockchain applications using test assets rather than ordinary mainnet assets. Introduce the idea of learning before interacting with unfamiliar applications.

**Suggested call to action:** What would you like to learn first about testnets?

### Day 2 — The Faucet Refresh Loop

Create a lighthearted meme about repeatedly refreshing a faucet page while waiting for test tokens.

**Key message:** Testnet exploration takes patience. Repeated refreshing does not guarantee that tokens will arrive.

### Day 3 — Wallet Safety Before Your First Test

Prepare a concise checklist covering wallet security, network verification, official links, and the importance of never sharing a recovery phrase or private key.

**Suggested call to action:** Which wallet-safety check do you always perform first?

### Day 4 — My First Testnet Mistake

Use a clearly labeled fictional example or a real, documented personal experience to explain a common beginner mistake.

**Key message:** Mistakes can become useful lessons when they are documented honestly.

**Editorial note:** Do not present a fictional story as a real personal experience.

### Day 5 — What Is a Testnet Faucet?

Explain what a faucet does, why test tokens may be limited, and why faucet availability can vary.

**Suggested call to action:** Which part of using a faucet feels most confusing to you?

### Day 6 — When the Testnet Transaction Is Still Pending

Create a meme about waiting for a test transaction to complete.

**Key message:** A pending transaction is a reason to check its status and relevant network information, not to assume it has failed or succeeded.

### Day 7 — What Did You Learn This Week?

Publish a weekly reflection prompt inviting beginners to identify one new concept, one challenge, and one question they still have.

**Suggested call to action:** Share one testnet lesson you would tell a beginner.

## Content Distribution

The seven planned content pieces follow the campaign's agreed content pillars:

- **Learn:** 3 pieces
- **Laugh:** 2 pieces
- **Share:** 2 pieces

This distribution is a practical seven-day implementation of the campaign strategy. The content pillars remain the strategic guide, with Learn receiving slightly more space because the target audience is new to Web3.

## Measurement Plan

For each published content piece, record the following when reliable data is available:

- Publication date and content link.
- Impressions or views, if the platform provides them.
- Relevant comments, replies, or feedback.
- Questions that reveal confusion or understanding.
- Lessons that could improve the next piece of content.

Because this is a portfolio simulation, no publication or engagement results are claimed here. The calendar represents planned content, not proof that the content has been published.

## Editorial Principles

- Use beginner-friendly language.
- Keep educational claims accurate and easy to verify.
- Do not promise airdrops, token allocations, or financial returns.
- Label fictional examples clearly.
- Protect wallet credentials and encourage careful link verification.
- Treat feedback as an opportunity to improve future educational material.
